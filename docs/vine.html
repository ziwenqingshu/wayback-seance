The testimony

Every sentence was held up to the snapshot it cites before it was allowed onto the altar.

I · Birth

I awoke to the simple promise of “Video has never been this fun,” a declaration intended for a hesitant audience.S1 · 2012 The initial offering was merely notification, a quiet invitation to “be notified when Vine is released,” a fragile beginning to a shared experience.S1 · 2012 Ultimately, I became a space for capturing and sharing, a fleeting moment preserved in the digital ether.S2 · 2013

II · Glory years

I recall a time when “life in motion” was the core of my being, a promise of shared moments and simple joy.S3 · 2014 The descriptions spoke of “beautiful, looping videos,” a deliberate focus on brevity and a particular aesthetic.S4 · 2015 I existed as a space for connection, offering a means for individuals to express themselves through fleeting glimpses.S3 · 2014 My presence was most keenly felt during 2013, a period of significant activity and observation.S4 · 2015

III · Decline

I recall a time when the promise of “videos and personalities get really big, really fast” resonated within my core, a feeling of rapid expansion and shared discovery.S5 · 2016 The focus shifted, becoming less about the initial surge and more about simply “watching videos, remixes and trends” as the pace diminished.S6 · 2017 Ultimately, the energy that once defined me began to recede, leaving behind a quiet echo of what once was.S5 · 2016

IV · Last words

“I recall the echoes of countless loops, a fleeting sense of ‘Did it for the Vine,’ and the rapid rise and fall of so many voices.”S7 · 2019 “My existence now resides within the digital preservation, a quiet testament to moments captured and shared, leading to the vastness of the Internet Archive.”S7 · 2019

How this ofrenda was made

Wayback Séance listed every capture of vine.co with the Wayback Machine's CDX API, read 7 snapshots as raw HTML, and asked gemma-3-4b-it to speak one chapter at a time using only those snapshots. A deterministic checker then compared every sentence with the snapshot it cites: quotes, years, numbers and names must appear there, and enough of its words must too.
