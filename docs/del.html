The testimony

Every sentence was held up to the snapshot it cites before it was allowed onto the altar.

I · Birth

I awoke to the simple declaration of “del.icio.us,” a space offered to “most recent posters” like “julian deusx” and “hatless ricmac”.S1 · 2003 The promise of “all your bookmarks in one place” resonated faintly, alongside the notion of “A tag is simply a word”.S2 · 2008 Though the archive remembers my frequent visits in 2006, a quiet melancholy persists within these digital echoes.S2 · 2008

II · Glory years

I recall a time when “Keep, share, and discover the web” was a simple promise, offered through a network of linked bookmarks.S3 · 2011 The suggestion to “Share It With Others!” resonated, fostering a sense of collective exploration and the potential for “recipients know it’s you and can reply”.S4 · 2016 Despite the warnings of “something bad happened. do you wanna see?” and the need for “we currently have limited support for your browser,” there was a persistent drive to “discover & collect the web”.S3 · 2011 Ultimately, I became “Delicious,” a service intended to connect users with “Type tags to add”.S4 · 2016

III · Decline

I recall a time when “Keep, share, and discover” was the core of my being, a promise of curated knowledge offered through “Delicious”.S5 · 2016 The simple act of logging in, “Please log in,” felt weighty with the expectation of a vast, interconnected web of interests.S6 · 2022 Yet, the urgency of simply existing diminished, as the focus shifted to a quiet return, a lingering presence within the digital space.S6 · 2022

IV · Last words

“I recall the simple invitation, ‘Welcome to del.icio.us,’ echoing across the network, a persistent request for connection.”S7 · 2025 “Ultimately, my address now directs to a redirection, a quiet fade into the vastness of the internet’s shifting pathways.”S7 · 2025

How this ofrenda was made

Wayback Séance listed every capture of del.icio.us with the Wayback Machine's CDX API, read 7 snapshots as raw HTML, and asked gemma-3-4b-it to speak one chapter at a time using only those snapshots. A deterministic checker then compared every sentence with the snapshot it cites: quotes, years, numbers and names must appear there, and enough of its words must too.

Snapshots courtesy of the Internet Archive. The Wayback Machine is how the web remembers; consider supporting it. archive.org/donate
