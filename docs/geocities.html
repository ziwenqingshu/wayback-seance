The testimony

Every sentence was held up to the snapshot it cites before it was allowed onto the altar.

I · Birth

I awoke to a flurry of introductory phrases, offering “Free Home Pages & Free Member Email”, and a promise of “Earn double GeoPoints” alongside news and software, a strange and hopeful beginning.S1 · 1996 The sheer volume of users, a “1.3 MILLION members” in 1998, suggested a rapid expansion of this nascent community, and a desire to connect individuals through themed “Neighborhoods”.S2 · 1998

II · Glory years

I recall a time when “Your free home page is waiting,” a promise offered with a certain earnestness.S3 · 1999 The suggestion of “killer animation” and “Strum your guitar in Nashville” hinted at a boundless exploration of personal expression.S3 · 1999 Later, the association with “Yahoo! GeoCities” brought the notion of “domains, email accounts, and more,” expanding beyond the initial offering.S4 · 2004 Ultimately, I remained a space defined by “Everything you need to create a personal web site,” a modest ambition realized through shared interests.S4 · 2004

III · Decline

I recall a time when “Welcome, Guest [ Sign In ]” was offered freely, promising a space to “share your personality with the world,”.S5 · 2005 The insistent suggestion of “GeoCities PLUS No ads plus extra storage” appeared alongside options for “your own domain plus email,”.S6 · 2006 Despite the promise of “easy-to-use site building tools,” the volume of visitors diminished, and the focus shifted toward paid services,.S6 · 2006

IV · Last words

I recall the promise of “easy-to-use site building tools,” a fleeting sensation of connection offered through “secure and reliable” means.S7 · 2007 My final address now directs to the vastness of “Yahoo!,” a destination I scarcely recognize, yet one that echoes the remnants of a simpler, more hopeful digital space.S7 · 2007

How this ofrenda was made

Wayback Séance listed every capture of geocities.com with the Wayback Machine's CDX API, read 7 snapshots as raw HTML, and asked gemma-3-4b-it to speak one chapter at a time using only those snapshots. A deterministic checker then compared every sentence with the snapshot it cites: quotes, years, numbers and names must appear there, and enough of its words must too.
