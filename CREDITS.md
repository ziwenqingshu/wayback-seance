# Credits

Wayback Séance stands on other people's open work:

- **[Internet Archive / Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/)**: every relic is a capture they kept. The CDX API and
  `id_` raw snapshots are what make this possible. [Please support them](https://archive.org/donate).
- **[Gemma 3](https://ai.google.dev/gemma)** by Google DeepMind (open weights, [Gemma Terms of Use](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms)),
  used as the GGUF conversion [`unsloth/gemma-3-4b-it-GGUF`](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/gemma-3-4b-it-GGUF) (Q4_0).
- **[llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)** (MIT): `llama-server` runs the model on a plain CPU.
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com/)** (MIT) and **[llama-cpp-python](https://github.com/abetlen/llama-cpp-python)** (MIT): optional backends.
- **[asciinema](https://asciinema.org/)** and **[agg](https://github.com/asciinema/agg)**: the terminal recording.

The sites in the demo (GeoCities, del.icio.us, Vine) and their names belong to their owners. The ofrendas quote
short excerpts of archived public pages for commemoration and commentary, and link back to the archive for each one.
